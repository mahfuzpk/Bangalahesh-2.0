The Vision of Future

Bangladesh 2.0 aims to redefine the nation's trajectory by embracing cutting-edge technology, promoting inclusive growth, and ensuring sustainability. With solutions to problems through digital transformation, and robust infrastructure, the country is set to become a global model of development.



Digital Transformation

Creating a connected, digital ecosystem to ensure access to digital services for all citizens.

Green Energy

Implementing solar and wind energy to ensure sustainable energy availability.

Modern Infrastructure

Building state of the art infrastructure to support economic growth and development.
